Company

204Labs is an independent software company based in Sharjah, United Arab Emirates.

204Labs FZE creates and owns Rewizz, bAIsect, Tether, Lurniq and FlatIntel. Each product has its own direction, audience and stage of development, with 204Labs providing the foundation behind the work.

Useful ideas deserve a serious attempt.

204Labs looks for problems worth solving, explores ideas that others might overlook, and builds the ones that can become useful, human-centred software products.

The company is registered as 204Labs FZE in SPC Free Zone, Sharjah, United Arab Emirates, under licence number 4432199.
