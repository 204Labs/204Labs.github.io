1 Scope

This Cookie Policy explains how 204Labs FZE uses cookies and similar technologies on our websites, applications, software, and related online services. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

2 What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small text files placed on a browser or device when a person visits a website or uses an online service. Similar technologies include pixels, tags, scripts, local storage, session storage, software development kits, device identifiers, and server logs.

These technologies may collect or store information such as device details, IP address, browser type, pages viewed, buttons clicked, session identifiers, account state, preferences, approximate location, and interactions with emails or service features.

3 Why we use these technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Operate the services and keep core functions working.
  • Authenticate users, maintain sessions, remember security choices, and prevent fraud or misuse.
  • Remember preferences, such as language, region, display, and consent settings.
  • Understand how the services are used and where performance, reliability, or design can be improved.
  • Measure the effectiveness of communications, product pages, and marketing campaigns where permitted.
  • Support integrations, embedded content, analytics, payment flows, and other third-party features chosen or enabled by us or by a customer.

5 First-party and third-party technologies

First-party technologies are set by or for 204Labs FZE. Third-party technologies are set by service providers or integrated platforms, such as hosting providers, analytics providers, payment processors, support tools, content delivery networks, communication tools, or embedded service providers.

Third parties may process information under their own privacy and cookie policies. We do not control third-party technologies after they are placed or operated by the relevant third party, except where our agreement with that third party provides otherwise.

6 Consent and choices

Where applicable law requires consent for non-essential cookies or similar technologies, we will request consent before using those technologies and provide a way to withdraw or change consent.

Users can manage cookies through browser settings. Most browsers allow users to block cookies, delete existing cookies, or receive alerts before cookies are placed. Blocking cookies may cause parts of the services to stop working or to lose saved preferences.

Some devices and browsers offer privacy signals or settings that limit tracking. We will respond to legally required signals where applicable and technically feasible.

7 Retention

Session cookies usually expire when a browser session ends. Persistent cookies and similar technologies remain for a defined period unless deleted earlier. Retention periods vary depending on the purpose of the technology, user settings, customer settings, and legal or security requirements.

8 Updates to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The updated version will be effective when posted or otherwise made available, unless a later effective date is stated.
