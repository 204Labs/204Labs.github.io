A few things you might be wondering.

Is Lurniq available now?

Not yet. Lurniq is in development for Android and iOS. Contact us for updates about early access and launch availability.

Can I use it without connecting my email?

Yes. Lurniq Free is designed to work without an account or mailbox connection. You can enter school information yourself and keep it organised on your phone.

What if my children attend different schools?

That is part of the core design. Lurniq brings each child's dates and tasks into one combined view while keeping the individual school information separate.

Will it connect to every school portal?

No. Lurniq Plus will support selected school portals and email connections. Availability depends on each provider. You can still add items manually, and Plus will offer other supported ways to bring information in.

Can I pay school fees in Lurniq?

No. Lurniq helps you track fees and record when you have paid them. Payments are made directly to the school or provider.

What happens to my information if I stop Plus?

The plan is that information already saved stays available to read, edit and export. Automatic collection and cloud features stop when the subscription ends.
