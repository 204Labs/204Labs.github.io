1 Who Lurniq is for

Lurniq helps adult parents, legal guardians and authorised caregivers organise a child's school life. Lurniq is for adults aged 18 or over. Children do not create or operate Lurniq accounts in this version.

An adult may use Lurniq to store a child's name, school, timetable, events, activities, fees, contacts, documents and school communications. This means that Lurniq handles children's personal information even though children do not use the app.

2 Lurniq Free

Lurniq Free does not require an account. The parent enters information manually. Readable household records are stored in the encrypted Lurniq database on the parent's phone. A parent may create an encrypted backup file and save it with a provider of their choice. 204Labs does not receive the readable contents of that backup.

3 Information we handle

The information depends on the features a parent uses.

  • Adult information includes the adult's email address, adult and caregiver confirmation, settings, support messages, accepted terms and privacy versions.
  • Child information includes a child's name, school, year or class, the school's registration number for the child if the adult adds it, the child's school email address if the adult adds it, timetable, events, activities, actions, fees, contacts, documents, photographs and school communications.
  • Source information includes uploaded or shared files, pasted text and the structured information produced from those sources.
  • Other-person information may include teachers, coaches, school staff, family members and people mentioned in a school communication.
  • Apple and Google may separately receive store or operating-system diagnostics under their own terms.

School communications can contain incidental health, disability, dietary, custody or safeguarding information. Lurniq does not create a health or safeguarding profile.

4 Why we use information

204Labs uses information to:

  • provide the Free functions selected by the parent;
  • organise, search, remind, back up, export, correct and delete records;
  • convert source material into proposed dates, actions, fees, contacts or summaries for the parent to check;
  • investigate incidents;
  • respond to support and privacy requests.

The legal basis is the adult's consent where consent is required, including the installation-level parental consent described below. Processing that is objectively necessary to provide a feature requested by the adult is also carried out for that service. 204Labs may process limited information to meet a UAE legal obligation or protect legal rights.

204Labs does not sell household information, display advertising, use child information for targeted advertising, or use Lurniq user information to train or improve a general-purpose artificial intelligence model.

5 Parent or legal guardian authority and consent

Lurniq is for adults. Children do not create or independently operate Lurniq accounts.

During initial setup, the adult must give one explicit confirmation stating:

I confirm that I am the parent, legal guardian or otherwise legally authorised caregiver of every child whose information I add to Lurniq. I expressly consent, on behalf of every child under 13 whom I am legally authorised to add, to 204Labs FZE processing that child's information as described in the Lurniq Privacy Notice.

The confirmation is not pre-selected. Lurniq records the local installation, the confirmation wording and Privacy Notice versions, the date and time, and the adult's affirmative action. Lurniq does not ask a child to consent and does not collect identity documents by default.

This confirmation covers children whom the adult is legally authorised to add during setup and later. The adult is not asked to complete another consent checkbox whenever a child is added. When the adult selects Add child, Lurniq records the child-profile identifier and creation time and associates that profile with the existing parent-authority and consent record.

The adult must only add a child if they are the child's parent, legal guardian or otherwise legally authorised caregiver. Providing another person's child's information without lawful authority violates the Terms. 204Labs may reasonably rely on the adult's recorded declaration unless it becomes aware of circumstances that call it into question.

The adult may withdraw permission for one child in Privacy and Data by removing that child. Backup files saved independently by the adult remain under the adult's control until the adult deletes them.

If a person believes that a child was added without authority, they may contact privacy@204labs.com. An adult's false declaration does not remove 204Labs' duties under applicable privacy and child-protection law.

6 What happens in Lurniq Free

Readable household content stays on the phone.

An encrypted backup file is created on the phone before the adult saves it. The selected storage provider may process the encrypted file under its own terms. 204Labs cannot read the file or recover it if the adult loses both the device and the recovery password or key.

9 Who receives information

204Labs discloses information only where needed to:

  • contracted communications service providers;
  • Google for app distribution and platform diagnostics;
  • professional advisers and auditors under confidentiality;
  • a competent authority where disclosure is required or permitted by law; or
  • protect a child or another person where a lawful safeguarding response is necessary.

Service providers must act under written terms that limit purpose, access, retention, onward processing and use of Lurniq information. 204Labs does not give a school access to a family's Lurniq records merely because the family selects that school.

10 Processing outside the UAE

For each cross-border service, 204Labs records the recipient, country, information, purpose and retention, signs data-protection terms requiring UAE-law-equivalent safeguards, limits access and uses encryption and minimisation. Transfers are made under Articles 22 or 23 of the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, as applicable.

12 Rights and choices

The adult can correct records, export information, delete a child profile and delete local information in the app.

Depending on UAE law and the request, a person may ask for information, access, a portable copy, correction, erasure, restriction, cessation of processing, objection to marketing and human review of a materially affecting automated result. Contact privacy@204labs.com. 204Labs may request proportionate proof of identity and authority to protect the family and other people.

204Labs will acknowledge a privacy request within three business days and aims to complete it within 30 days. If 204Labs does not hold readable Free data or cannot decrypt a backup, it will explain that limitation and give device-level instructions.

A person may complain to the competent UAE data protection authority. 204Labs will provide the current authority route on request.

14 Security and incidents

204Labs uses encryption, least-privilege access, backup and restore testing, secure deletion and service-provider review. No service can promise perfect security.

If a breach is likely to prejudice privacy, confidentiality or security, 204Labs will notify the competent UAE authority and affected people promptly as required by law. UAE law does not state a general 72-hour deadline in the primary legislation.

15 Changes and contact

204Labs will update the version and effective date when this notice changes. A material new purpose, data recipient, processing country, child-access model, social feature or use of readable off-device information requires notice before the change takes effect and new consent where required.
