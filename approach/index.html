Experimentation

Try things. Learn. Improve. Try again.

Good products rarely arrive fully formed. 204Labs creates room to test ideas, question assumptions and learn from what does not work.

Curiosity with Purpose

Ask questions, but know why you are asking them.

Curiosity is used to find overlooked gaps, awkward problems and small frustrations that can become useful software when handled with purpose.

Human

People come before features.

Human means thinking about both the people who use a product and the people who create it.

Optimism

Assume there is a way forward.

Optimism means approaching problems believing they can be understood, challenged and improved.
