Game Play Preview

Recorded gameplay. Preview opens in YouTube.

Quick to pick up. Hard to put down.

Drag to steer. Tap a side of the screen to throw. Flick up to jump, flick down to duck, and swipe to turn when the road ends.

  • A run that never ends. Monday to Sunday and into the next week, each street longer, faster and busier than the last. Every run starts somewhere new.
  • Mailbox combos. Mailbox hits build your combo. Five in a row start a Mail Rush, where every delivery counts double.
  • A busy street. Cars, buses, dogs, skaters and market stalls share the road. Near misses earn points; a crash costs a heart.
  • Three power-ups. The Golden Paper doubles your points, the Speed Autopilot takes the handlebars, and the Magic Vest takes the next crash for you.
  • Goals, medals and ranks. Three new goals every day, a medal for each day of the week, feat badges and eleven carrier ranks.
  • Side quests. Today's Daily Route is the same street for every player. Paper Marksman is mailboxes only.
  • World and country leaderboards. Coming in the next update: see where your best run, distance, longest streak and Daily Route score rank against the world and your own country, today, this week or of all time. Your rank, and how far it moved, shows after every run.

Questions

Where can I play it?

Paper Run is on Google Play for Android phones and tablets. The iPhone and iPad version is in testing and coming soon to the App Store.

Is it free?

Yes. It is free to play, with ads and optional in-app purchases. "Remove ads" turns off the ads between runs. Nothing you buy makes a street easier or harder.

Who is it for?

Paper Run is made for players aged 13 and up.

Do I need an account?

No. Your progress is saved on your phone. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy is kept in your own Google account so it comes back on a new phone. From the next update the leaderboards show a player name, your country's flag and your scores to other players, with no sign-up. The Paper Run privacy policy explains how the leaderboards, ads, purchases and reports handle data.

Where can I follow Paper Run?

On YouTube and Instagram, as @playpaperrun.
