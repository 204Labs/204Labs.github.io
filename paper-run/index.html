Quick to pick up. Hard to put down.

Each run is a single street and a few minutes long. Clear it, earn your stars, and the next morning brings a bigger, busier street.

A new street every day

Seven streets from Monday to Sunday, each with its own houses, residents and traffic, and each one faster than the last.

Swipe, jump, duck, turn

Steer with one thumb, flick up to jump and down to duck, and swipe left or right to take the turn at each junction.

Land it on the porch

Tap a side of the screen to throw. Hit subscribers' porches and mailboxes, skip the houses that never ordered, and chase golden papers for bonus points.

Mind the neighbourhood

Cars, buses, dogs, cats, skaters and puddles all share the road. Near misses earn points; collisions cost a heart.

Residents who remember you

People on your route cheer a clean delivery, grumble at a missed one and shout when you come too close.

A hand-made cartoon world

A stylised 3D suburb with swaying trees and puffy clouds, set to original music, sound effects and voices.

How to play

  1. Steer and dodge

    Drag anywhere to steer. Flick up to jump, flick down to duck.

  2. Throw

    Tap the left or right half of the screen to throw a paper to that side of the street.

  3. Take the turn

    When the road splits, swipe left or right before the junction, or Kit rides straight into a house.

Where can I play it?

Paper Run 3D is coming first to Android phones, on Google Play.

Is it free?

Yes. It is free to play, with optional in-app purchases and ads. You can buy "Remove ads" to turn off the ads between runs.

Who is it for?

Paper Run 3D is made for players aged 13 and up.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in, and your progress is saved on your phone. Read the Paper Run 3D privacy policy for how ads and purchases handle data.
