Paper Run – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Who we are

Paper Run is a mobile game for Android, iPhone and iPad made by 204 Labs ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Paper Run app on Google Play and on Apple's App Store.

Questions or requests about your privacy: connect@204labs.com.

What the game keeps on your phone

Paper Run has no account for you to make. Your progress (days cleared, scores, unlocked outfits, coins, settings) is saved on your device. Apart from the leaderboard entries described below, we do not receive it.

We do not collect your email address, contacts, photos, precise location or microphone audio.

Google Play may share with us statistics it gathers itself, such as crash reports and install counts, according to your device settings and Google's own policies.

Leaderboards

Paper Run has world and country leaderboards for the best run, the distance ridden, the longest delivery streak and the Daily Route, for today, this week and of all time. They work the same in every country and need no sign-up.

When you finish a run with a score better than your own best on a board, the game sends that entry to our leaderboard service. Each entry holds:

  • a player name: your Google Play Games name if you are signed in to Play Games on Android, otherwise "RIDER" and four letters
  • a two-letter country code, taken from the region in your phone's language settings (not from your location)
  • your score for that board, and the board's name (which board, and which day or week)
  • a random ID for this install of the game, made when the game first connects. It is not your name, your Google account or your Apple ID

Anyone who plays Paper Run can see the leaderboards: the player name, the country flag, the score and the rank. The random ID is not shown.

The leaderboards use Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication (anonymous sign-in), Google services that we use as our service provider. Cloud Firestore keeps the entries in the European Union. Data travels to them over encrypted connections, and the service's rules let each install write only its own entries, and only to raise a score.

We use the entries only to show the leaderboards and your rank. We do not use them for advertising, and we do not sell them.

Cloud save with Google Play Games

Where the game offers cloud save and you are signed in to Google Play Games on your phone, the game also keeps a copy of your saved game in your own Google Play Games account. This is so your progress, and anything you bought, comes back after a reinstall or on a new phone.

That copy holds the same gameplay information as the save on your phone and nothing else. Google stores it under your Google account, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive it and cannot read it. If you are not signed in to Google Play Games, no copy is made and the game plays the same.

Usage and crash reports

To learn how the game is played and to fix crashes, Paper Run uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, two Google services.

They are switched on only in countries where the game does not need to ask for your consent to ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland they stay off.

Where they are on, they collect:

  • how you play: screens opened, runs started and finished, scores and how far you got, continues, adverts watched for a reward, and purchases
  • the game's version, your device model, operating system and language, and the country your IP address suggests
  • a random ID for this install of the game, which is not your name or your Google account
  • if the game crashes or hits an error: what it was doing, and the state of the device at that moment

We use this only to improve and fix the game, never for advertising or to identify you. Google processes it for us as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Firebase privacy information.

Advertising

Paper Run shows ads through Google AdMob, Google's advertising service. Some ads are optional: you choose to watch them for a reward. Others appear between rounds unless you buy "Remove ads".

To show and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect from your device:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
  • your IP address and the approximate location it suggests
  • how you interact with ads

Google uses this as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change that choice in the game's settings.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on your phone).

In-app purchases

Purchases in Paper Run on Android (Remove ads, Coin doubler and coin packs) are made through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or bank details.

For each purchase, Google gives the game an order number and a purchase token so it can unlock what you bought and restore it if you reinstall. Google Play's handling of your payment information is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

On iPhone and iPad

On iPhone and iPad, Paper Run uses the leaderboards described above. It shows no ads, has no in-app purchases or cloud save, and sends no usage or crash reports.

Children

Paper Run is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your saved game stays on your phone until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Uninstalling deletes it.

A cloud save copy stays in your Google Play Games account until you delete it. To delete it, open the Google Play Games app, go to Settings, tap "Delete Play Games account & data", find Paper Run under "Delete individual game data" and tap Delete.

Leaderboard entries stay on their boards until we remove them. Uninstalling the game does not remove them. To have your entries removed, email connect@204labs.com with the player name shown on the board and your country, and we will delete them.

Usage and crash reports are kept by Google under the retention settings of our Firebase project. Advertising data is kept by Google under its own policies. To ask about or delete anything we hold, email connect@204labs.com and we will reply within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, and to object to or limit its use for advertising. We do not sell your personal information. To use any of these rights, email connect@204labs.com.

Security

The game has no servers of its own. The leaderboards, purchases, ads, cloud save and usage and crash reports use Google's services, which encrypt data in transit.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's update notes.
