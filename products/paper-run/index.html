Paper Run: deliver the morning paper, dodge everything else.

An endless arcade game for Android. Ride Kit's scooter through seven suburbs and land the morning paper in every mailbox, one street a day, week after week, until the last heart is gone.

Explore Paper Run

Who it is for

Players aged 13 and up who enjoy quick, colourful arcade runs that can be mastered one street at a time.

How it plays

Drag to steer, flick up to jump and down to duck, swipe to take the turn at each junction, and tap a side of the screen to throw. Mailbox hits build a combo, five in a row start a Mail Rush, and each day is a longer, faster street. There are three new goals every day, medals for every day of the week and two side quests.

Availability

Paper Run 1.0.0 is on Google Play for Android. It is free to play, with ads and optional in-app purchases.

Privacy

Paper Run has no account of its own, and progress is saved on the player's phone, with an optional copy in the player's own Google Play Games account. The Paper Run privacy policy explains how ads, purchases and usage reports handle data.
