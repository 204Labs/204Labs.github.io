In development / 204Labs product

FlatIntel helps renters know more before signing the lease.

Renters often discover what a building is really like only after moving in. FlatIntel is being built for structured, building-level rental insight.

What it addresses

FlatIntel is intended for renters who want clearer building-level insight before committing to a lease.

At a high level, FlatIntel is being built to turn verified tenant experience into structured building-level insight, while giving owners and property managers a fair way to respond, explain and show what has improved.

Privacy and control

The current public description supports a product direction centred on verified tenant experience and fair response mechanisms.
