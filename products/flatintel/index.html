FlatIntel helps renters know more before signing the lease.

Renters usually learn what a building is like after moving in. FlatIntel turns verified tenant experience into building-level insight, and gives owners a fair way to respond.

Who it is for

Renters who want building-level insight before committing to a lease.

How it works

FlatIntel is being built to turn verified tenant experience into structured, building-level insight. Owners and property managers get a fair way to respond, explain and show what has improved.

Availability

FlatIntel is being built and is not yet open to the public.
