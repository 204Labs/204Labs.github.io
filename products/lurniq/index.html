Lurniq brings school life back into one place.

Lurniq is being built to bring the information parents actually need into one clear view across children and schools.

What it addresses

Parents often have to track emails, portals, timetables, PDFs, fees, activities and reminders across different places.

Lurniq is intended as a parent school organiser that keeps original sources visible and the parent firmly in control. AI works quietly in the background where it is useful; it is not the product.

Privacy and control

The current public description emphasises source visibility and parent control as core product principles.
