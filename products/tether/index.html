Tether is a calmer way to run family life.

Family life rarely fits neatly into a calendar. Tether is designed around busy, often messy households rather than an idealised family routine.

What it addresses

Tether is intended for families that need a calmer way to coordinate schedules, routines, missions, meals, lists, reading and family communication.

At a high level, it brings those moving parts into one privacy-first family space.

Privacy and control

The current site-supported description frames Tether as privacy-first and built around family coordination.
