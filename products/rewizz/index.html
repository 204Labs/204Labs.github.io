Rewizz helps teachers get their time back.

Teachers already have the material. Rewizz helps turn existing curriculum and lesson content into useful revision packs without rebuilding everything from scratch.

Visit Rewizz ↗Join the beta ↗

What it addresses

Rewizz is intended for teachers who already have lesson material and need a more efficient way to prepare revision packs.

At a high level, Rewizz is designed to let the teacher choose existing curriculum or lesson content, review what is selected, adjust it and export the result.

Privacy and control

Core processing is designed to stay local by default, with the teacher deciding what is selected, changed and ultimately exported.
