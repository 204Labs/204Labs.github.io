## Titles And Metadata

The homepage already had a descriptive title, meta description, Open Graph image metadata, Twitter image metadata and canonical URL. The policy pages had canonical URLs and descriptions. Before this work, product and entity pages did not exist as crawlable canonical pages.

## Canonicals

Canonical URLs use `https://www.204labs.com/`. The homepage canonical was already correct. The new entity pages use folder URLs with trailing slashes.

## Headings

The homepage uses one visible `h1`. Policy pages use one `h1`. The new entity/product pages are required to keep one `h1` each.

## Structured Data

Existing homepage schema covered:

- `Organization`
- `WebSite`
- `WebPage`
- `ItemList`

Baseline gaps:

- Product `ItemList` URLs pointed to homepage anchors rather than durable product URLs.
- Product pages had no individual `WebPage`, `BreadcrumbList` or application-level schema because those pages did not exist.
- `/notes/` did not exist as a `CollectionPage`.

## Robots And Sitemap

Baseline `robots.txt` was permissive for all crawlers and referenced the sitemap. Baseline `sitemap.xml` included only the homepage and legal pages.

## Internal Links

The homepage had product disclosures and product actions, but there were no canonical product pages to link to. Legal links existed in the footer.

## Response Codes

Observed before this work:

- `https://www.204labs.com/` served from GitHub Pages.
- HTTPS was fixed before this baseline.
- New URL response codes will be validated after deployment.

## Performance

The site is static HTML/CSS/JS with local font files and no framework build. Main performance risks are animation cost in the ECHO and outlook sections, web font loading, image sizes and third-party analytics after consent.

## Discoverability

Baseline discoverability was limited by the absence of canonical company, product and notes URLs. The homepage could describe the product portfolio, but search engines and answer engines had no durable URL per product.

## Known Baseline Limitations

- No live Lighthouse capture is included in this baseline.
- No search console indexing state is included in the repo.
- Product facts are intentionally limited to current public site/repo-supported information.
