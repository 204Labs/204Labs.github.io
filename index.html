02 / Our approachSkip ECHO ↓

E C H O

ECHO is the ethos behind how we explore ideas, build products and respond when things do not go according to plan.

Experimentation

Try things. Learn. Improve. Try again.

E Try. Learn. Improve. Try again.

We believe good products rarely arrive fully formed. We create room to test ideas, question assumptions and get things wrong without treating failure as something to fear.

We like the principle behind the line often attributed to Nelson Mandela: I never lose. I either win or learn.

The learning matters just as much as the outcome.

Curiosity with Purpose

Ask questions, but know why you are asking them.

C

We encourage curiosity, exploration and looking beyond the obvious. That means finding the awkward gaps, overlooked problems and small frustrations that others have learned to live with.

But curiosity alone is not enough. We want to turn it into something useful.

Human

People come before features.

H

The people
who use it.

The people
who build it.

People come
before features.

We build for humans, not specifications.

That means thinking not only about the people who use our products, but also the people who create them. We want our products to be clear, useful and enjoyable for users, while also building a culture where curiosity, respect, balance and good thinking can thrive.

The best products are usually made by people who feel trusted, supported and connected to the purpose behind what they are building.

Optimism

Assume there is a way forward.

O

Optimism does not mean pretending problems do not exist. It means approaching them believing they can be understood, challenged and improved.

That applies when an idea fails, when a technical problem appears, when plans change, and when we are deciding what to build next.

03 / The portfolio

Our products

01

Rewizz

CLOSED BETA+

Helping teachers get their time back.

Teachers already have the material. Rewizz helps turn existing curriculum and lesson content into useful revision packs without rebuilding everything from scratch. Core processing is designed to stay local by default, with the teacher deciding what is selected, changed and ultimately exported.

bAIsect

BUILDING V1+

For when building with AI gets you almost there.

AI can take a builder surprisingly far, until the problem becomes too specific, too technical or simply too stubborn. bAIsect turns that messy problem into a structured issue report, then helps connect the builder with engineers equipped to diagnose, fix, finish or improve the work.

Tether

PRIVATE BETA+

A calmer way to run family life.

Family life rarely fits neatly into a calendar. Tether brings schedules, routines, missions, meals, lists, reading and family communication into one calm, privacy-first space, designed around neurodivergent, busy and often messy households rather than an idealised family routine.

04

Lurniq

PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT+

School life, brought back into one place.

Emails. Portals. Timetables. PDFs. Fees. Activities. Reminders. Lurniq is being built to bring the information parents actually need into one clear view across children and schools, while keeping the original source visible and the parent firmly in control.

AI works quietly in the background where it is useful. It is not the product.
